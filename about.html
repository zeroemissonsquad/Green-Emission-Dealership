About Our Company

Behind the scenes of the EV dealership.

ABOUT US

We are Green-Emission Dealership, a company where renting and purchasing electric and hydrogen vehicles are made easy. We sell electric and hydrogen automotives ranging from cars to motorbikes from various brands such as BMW, Tesla and Honda available on our website for purchase or rent.

OUR GOALS

Our main goal is to make renting and owning an electric and hydrogen vehicle quick, affordable and easy, so many people can own one without any trouble. Electric and hydrogen vehicles being affordable and trivial to obtain also allows for a better future of the planet, reducing green-house gasses and slowing down global warming and pollution.
